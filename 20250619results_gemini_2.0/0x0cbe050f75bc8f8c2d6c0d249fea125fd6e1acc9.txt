    *   This is a classic reentrancy vulnerability. If the contract at address `a` contains malicious code, it could call back into the `Caller` contract (or any other contract the caller interacts with) during the `call()` execution, potentially leading to unexpected state changes.
    *   Specifically, it is a "call to the unknown" as the target contract is arbitrary.
